Information We Collect

When you actively trigger the AI generation feature, the Extension collects the text you enter in the input field, including any “@” commands you may include. We collect only the minimum amount of data necessary to provide the core service.

We do not collect any personally identifiable information, such as your name, email address, physical location, or device identifiers. The Extension uses an anonymous, randomly generated identifier for service calls. This identifier cannot be traced back to your real identity.

How We Use Information

Your input text is securely transmitted to our backend server (AWS Lambda) via an encrypted channel (HTTPS) solely for the purpose of generating AI replies. The output is then returned to your browser’s input field over the same encrypted channel.

We do not use your input text for any other purposes, including but not limited to:

  • Training AI models
  • User behavior analytics or profiling
  • Advertising

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise share your input text with any third parties. The only communication is with the backend service hosted on AWS Lambda, and that service is used exclusively for AI generation.

Data Security

We employ industry-standard security measures to protect your data. All communications with the server are encrypted using HTTPS to prevent interception or tampering during transmission. Our backend processing script does not record or store your input text in any persistent storage after generation is complete.

Data Retention

Your input text is processed only temporarily in memory and is discarded immediately after the response is generated. We do not retain any history of your inputs or generated content in any form. This means that once processing is finished, we cannot recover or provide any of your past text data.

Your Rights

Because we do not retain any personal data or input content, you do not need to exercise rights to access, correct, or delete data. However, if you have any concerns or questions, you may contact us at any time using the information below, and we will do our best to address them.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically for the latest information.
